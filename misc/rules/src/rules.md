### Rules

1. Game announcements will be made on Discord: https://discord.gg/EDv3JqRHUZ
1. Flag format is `pdx{...}` unless otherwise noted.
1. When submitting a flag, make sure to submit the entire flag, not just the text inside the braces.
1. Please be respectful and inclusive of admins and fellow players. Remember, we are all trying to have the best CTF experience possible.
1. All players are subject to the BSIDESPDX Code of Conduct, the Portland State Code of Conduct, and Portland State OIT rules.
1. Please do not cheat. Penalties are at the discretion of the CTF organizers and are final.
1. Using AI to drive the solution is considered cheating. This CTF is designed for humans, to be fun, and to learn from. Using AI to research general topic areas ("how does MD5 work?") is fine. If you ever hand any part of the challenge directly to an AI, you're no longer playing the CTF. We expect teams to understand a challenge, their solution, and to have gotten a flag on their own. Carefully consider how your AI use impacts everyone else's experience of the CTF. Are you or the AI playing the challenge?
1. No flag sharing. Only lame people share flags, and by playing in this CTF you are automatically awesome.
1. You must be present at the Closing Remarks on Saturday to qualify for prizes. We are handing them out in person, they will not be mailed.
1. The scoreboard at ^https://ctf.bsidespd.xyz/.*$ is out of scope, and shouldn't be attacked.
1. No brute forcing using automated scanners (including AI agent brute force attempts). Please don't make us put POWs in front of all the challenges. If a challenge requires bruteforcing/automated enumeration, it will be explicitly noted in the challenge description.
1. If a vulnerability in the scoreboard or challenge infrastructure is identified, please privately message an admin on Discord. Please don't deliberately attack/abuse the challenge infrastructure.
1. If a host is determined to be bruteforcing and/or abusing infrastructure, the CTF organizers reserve the right to indefinitely block them from connecting to any infrastructure, without warning or notification. If you believe you've been blocked by mistake, please private message an admin.
1. These rules are subject to change, and any significant modifications will be announced.
